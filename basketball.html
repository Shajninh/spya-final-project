Football Comissioners: Jonas Tolliver - basketball@spya.org



We’re excited to kick off the Boys Travel and Rec Basketball Tryouts for the 2026/2027 season!


A Few Important Things

Trying out for Rec is totally optional. If your player skips a Rec tryout, no worries—they’ll still land on a team, and we’ll place them randomly. If your player wants a shot at Travel, there’s a $15 fee for the tryout. It’s non-refundable, even if they aren’t picked for a Travel team. Players who try for Travel but don’t make it don’t miss out—they’re automatically put on a Rec team. Just remember, we can’t refund that Travel tryout fee.
