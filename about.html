About SPYA

South Parkland Youth Association (SPYA) is a non-profit, volunteer-driven organization serving Allentown, PA and the surrounding Parkland community, providing quality sports programs for boys and girls ages 4 to 18.

Building Character, Teamwork, and Community Through Youth Sports

Our chief goal is to create a culture where kids love to participate in sports and feel proud to wear an SPYA uniform.

  • Volunteer-Led Mission: Run by dedicated local coaches and community members focused on athletic development, confidence, and leadership.
  • Inclusive Programs: Offering both recreational (intramural) and competitive travel programs tailored for all skill levels from ages 4 to 18.
  • Coach & Athlete Support: Providing in-house clinics, licensing opportunities, and subsidized training to maintain high coaching standards across all sports.

Spring and Fall development programs training young athletes in essential stick skills and game strategy.
