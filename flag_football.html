NFL Flag Football

Flag Football Comissioners: Sam Jones - flagfootball@spya.org



South Parkland Youth Association 5v5 Flag Football is a family friendly recreational program built on community and youth engagement for all skills levels. We offer 5 divisions for age groups 7u-15u. Each team plays 8-10 games per season plus at least 1 playoff game. Our Flag Football program is year round with seasons in the fall, winter, and spring. We also offer a skills program that falls between the fall and winter seasons.
